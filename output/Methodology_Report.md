### 1. The Core Tactical Constraints: 4-Overseas Quota & Auction Eligibility

#### 1.1 Matchday Quota & The "Indian Uncapped Scarcity Premium"
Under BCCI and IPL tournament rules:
1. **Matchday Playing XI Quota**: A team may field **at most 4 overseas players** in its starting XI. The remaining 7 positions must be occupied by Indian players.
2. **Impact Player Regulation**: If a team starts with 4 overseas players, the Impact Player substitute **must be an Indian player**.
3. **Auction Squad Limit**: A squad of 25 players allows a maximum of 8 overseas players and a minimum of 17 Indian players.

Because overseas talent across global leagues is abundant while top-tier Indian talent is tightly held by retentions, **every overseas slot spent on one position incurs an opportunity cost on another**:
- If RR deploys overseas players at #3 (Anchor) and #6 (Finisher), and retains Jofra Archer as its overseas strike pacer, **only ONE overseas slot remains**.
- If RR solves its death bowling with **Mohit Sharma (Indian Core)** and secures lower-order boundary hitting through **Shubham Dubey (Indian Uncapped)**, RR preserves its overseas slots for game-changing international spines like **Shai Hope**, **Dunith Wellalage**, or **Sean Abbott**.
- Uncapped Indian players also carry significantly lower auction price tags, preserving auction purse for targeted high-impact bidding wars.

#### 1.2 The BCCI & IPL Auction Eligibility Filter
Players are strictly evaluated for practical auction viability:
- **Exclusion of Pakistani & Pakistani-Origin Players**: Per BCCI policy, Pakistani cricketers and Pakistani-born expatriate players representing Associate nations (e.g., Yasim Murtaza from Hong Kong, Asif Khan from UAE) cannot enter the IPL auction register and are strictly filtered out.
- **Strict Overseas Age & Cap Filter**:
  - Overseas players with 0 IPL matches and age > 30 are eliminated.
  - Overseas players with 0 IPL registrations and age > 25 are eliminated; only raw uncapped prodigies under 22 years of age (Dunith Wellalage) are retained.
  - All other overseas acquisitions possess established IPL caps and match experience.
- **Exclusion of Underperforming Stop-Gaps**: Vijay Shankar is completely eliminated from the targeting model.

### 3. Continuous Pressure Index ($\text{PI}$) Formulation

To objectively evaluate player reliability, we constructed a continuous delivery-level **Pressure Index ($\text{PI} \in [0, 1]$)** evaluated at the instant before each ball is bowled:

$$\text{PI} = 0.15 \cdot F_{\text{phase}} + 0.20 \cdot F_{\text{wickets}} + 0.25 \cdot F_{\text{runrate}} + 0.15 \cdot F_{\text{stage}} + 0.25 \cdot F_{\text{closeness}}$$

Where:
1. **Match Phase ($F_{\text{phase}}$)**: Powerplay (overs 1–6) = $0.20$, Middle overs (7–15) = $0.50$, Death overs (16–20) = $1.00$.
2. **Wickets Resource Leverage ($F_{\text{wickets}}$)**: $F_{\text{wickets}} = \frac{W_{\text{lost}}}{9} \in [0, 1]$, reflecting batting resource exhaustion.
3. **Run Rate Urgency ($F_{\text{runrate}}$)**:
   - *Chasing (Innings 2)*: $F_{\text{runrate}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{\text{RRR} - 6.0}{14.0 - 6.0}, 0, 1\right)$
   - *Setting Target (Innings 1)*: $F_{\text{runrate}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{\text{CRR} - 6.0}{12.0 - 6.0}, 0, 1\right)$
4. **Tournament Stakes ($F_{\text{stage}}$)**: Playoffs, semi-finals, and finals = $1.00$; league/group stage matches = $0.40$.
5. **Score Closeness ($F_{\text{closeness}}$)**:
   - *Innings 2*: $F_{\text{closeness}} = 1.0 - \text{clip}\left(\frac{|\text{Runs Needed} - \text{Balls Remaining}|}{60}, 0, 1\right)$
   - *Innings 1*: Deviation of projected total from venue par score ($170$).

**Distribution Partitioning**:
- **Low Pressure**: $\text{PI} < 0.322$ (40.0% of balls)
- **Medium Pressure**: $0.322 \le \text{PI} < 0.494$ (36.2% of balls)
- **High Pressure (HP)**: $\text{PI} \ge 0.494$ (Top 23.8% highest-leverage deliveries; 47,446 balls)

### 4. Performance Scoring, Empirical Bayesian Shrinkage & Consistency

#### 4.1 Dimensional Uplift Metrics
We compare each player's performance under High Pressure ($\text{PI} \ge 0.494$) against their overall baseline:
- **Batters**:
  $$\text{PBS} = 0.35 \cdot \Delta\text{SR} + 0.30 \cdot \Delta\text{Avg} + 0.20 \cdot \Delta\text{Bdry}\% - 0.15 \cdot \Delta\text{Dot}\% + 0.10 \cdot \Delta\text{Impact}$$
- **Bowlers**:
  $$\text{PBoS} = 0.40 \cdot \Delta\text{Econ} + 0.30 \cdot \Delta\text{SR} + 0.15 \cdot \Delta\text{Dot}\% + 0.15 \cdot \Delta\text{Bdry}\% - 0.10 \cdot \Delta\text{Extras}\%$$

#### 4.2 Empirical Bayesian Shrinkage (Sample Size Noise Control)
To prevent small-sample distortions, we apply a Bayesian shrinkage multiplier:
$$\text{Multiplier}_{\text{Bayes}} = \frac{N_{\text{hp}}}{N_{\text{hp}} + K}$$
Where $K_{\text{bat}} = 40$ balls faced and $K_{\text{bowl}} = 50$ balls bowled.

#### 4.3 Match-to-Match Consistency Multiplier
To penalize erratic "boom-or-bust" performers and reward ice-cool reliability:
$$\text{Multiplier}_{\text{consistency}} = 0.75 + \frac{0.25}{1 + \text{CV}_{\text{match}}}$$
Where $\text{CV}_{\text{match}} = \frac{\sigma_{\text{match}}}{\mu_{\text{match}}}$ is the coefficient of variation across high-pressure match appearances.
